Analytics and session replay

Updated October 12, 2026.

We use Google Analytics to understand visits, devices, traffic sources, engagement, and signup outcomes. Outreach links may include an opaque referral ID. We store that referral in your browser to attribute later visits, and send it to Google Analytics. Analytics can use cookies and browser storage. Our custom interaction events do not include the email address you type, form content, or raw error messages.

Optional session replay

If you choose “Allow replay,” Microsoft Clarity collects interactions such as clicks, scrolling, page layout, and device information so we can find usability issues and build heatmaps. Clarity uses analytics cookies for session tracking. We send it signup outcome event names, but do not send email field values as custom tags or identifiers. The email input is explicitly masked, in addition to Clarity’s default masking. We send advertising-storage consent as denied.

Clarity does not load before you opt in. Choose “Replay privacy” on the website to change your choice. Choosing “No thanks” stops replay and, when already running, reloads the page without Clarity. Browser Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals disable replay. This choice controls Clarity only; Google Analytics is measured separately.

Submitting the signup form sends your email to our mailing-list provider, Loops, independently of analytics or replay. Declining replay does not prevent signup.

Learn more: Google privacy policy, Microsoft privacy statement, Clarity privacy information.
