One demo. No retraining.

We're testing the limits of DemoDiffusion: can one demo teach a frozen robot model a new task, with no training?

Limitations

  • It copies the demo, not the goal. With a demo from another task, orange into bowl went 0 of 8.
  • Gains are uneven. Bowl into sink works most of the time. The other three tasks succeed far less often.
  • Every new task needs its own demo. Nothing is learned for next time.
  • Simulation only, and small. 16 tries per setting, 49 for orange into bowl.

Future extensions

Directions we plan to try next. No results yet.

  • Exploration. Let the robot try variations of the demo on its own, so it finds what works beyond one example.
  • Reinforcement learning. Use those tries to train the skill into the model, so it no longer needs a demo.

Appendix

Setup

  • Kitchen scenes in Isaac Lab Arena, with a Franka arm.
  • Object positions are random on every try.
  • pi0.5's weights never change in the demo runs.
  • 16 tries per setting. 49 for orange into bowl.
  • Our demos were recorded robot runs. The animation shows a person to explain the idea.

How the demo is used

  • We fit the demo's path to where the objects are in this try.
  • pi0.5 normally builds each motion out of random noise, step by step.
  • Here it starts from the fitted demo path with some noise added, then finishes the motion as usual.

The nudge variant

  • A variant also nudges each step of that process toward the demo.
  • On bowl into sink: 9 of 16 without the nudge, 10 of 16 with it.
