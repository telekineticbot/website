The details

Setup

  • Kitchen scenes in Isaac Lab Arena, with a Franka arm.
  • Object positions are random on every try.
  • In the demo runs, pi0.5's weights never change.
  • 16 tries per setting. 49 for orange into bowl.

How the demo is used

  • We fit the demo's path to where the objects are in this try.
  • pi0.5 starts from that path plus noise, instead of from pure noise.
  • A variant also nudges each cleanup step toward the demo. On bowl into sink: 9 of 16 without the nudge, 10 of 16 with it.

What we take from it

  • One demo can unlock a task the model has never done.
  • It copies the demo, so the demo has to be the right one.
  • Training on 10 successful runs (about 40 minutes) made it stick: 30 of 32 with no demo.
  • The wrong demo test was on orange into bowl, with a demo from another task.
  • Next up: Vrishabh's work on having the robot explore on its own.
