An exploration on exploration

Proposal for safe real world robot exploration.

Vineeth YeevaniVrishabh KenkreNovember 18, 2026

This animation needs WebGL. The steps below tell the same story.

We want robots that improve themselves.

Give it a task it can't solve yet.

It has to explore to find a way.

Most methods wiggle around and pray.1

Wiggling rarely finds anything new.

Ours: blindfold it and give it the rough plan.

It fills in realistic motion on its own.

Many new tries. One works.
